01 · DATA PREPARATION

Data & cleaning

Import hourly observations, then review the daily averages and quality checks for training.

Import hourly data

Select a region and upload its EIA workbook or paste CSV or tab-separated rows with a header.

Supported columns and time zones

For EIA workbooks, the importer reads Published Hourly Data. It uses Local date, UTC time when present, and Demand. Text accepts Local date,Hour,Demand, UTC time,Demand, or timestamp,mw. If Region is present, rows are filtered to the selected region. ISO timestamps work best.

A supplied local date sets the aggregation day. For UTC-only input, dates are assigned in Pacific, Central, or Eastern time by region. For EIA hour-ending timestamps without local dates, select the checkbox.

Session sources

Build training series

Only days through TRAIN_END enter the preview and audit. Missing or nonpositive readings are excluded from hourly averages. DST dates use their actual 23- or 25-hour expectation.

02 · TRAINING ONLY

Training diagnostics

Inspect the daily series before comparing forecast models.

Training data only

Decomposition and stationarity

Weekly STL uses period 7. ACF and PACF inspect daily lags. ADF tests for a unit root, and KPSS tests level stationarity.

Build daily averages in Data & cleaning to begin.
03 · FORECAST SPECIFICATIONS

Forecast models

Four prespecified candidates share the same origin dates and observed targets.

Go to cross-validation
BENCHMARK

Seasonal naïve

Repeats the last seven available daily model inputs to forecast the corresponding day of the week.

period = 7
SMOOTHING

ETS

Estimates the level, a damped additive trend, and an additive weekly seasonal component.

ETS(A, Ad, A) · weekly seasonality
AUTOREGRESSION

ARIMA

Fits one autoregressive and one moving-average term after first differencing.

ARIMA(1, 1, 1)
CALENDAR REGRESSION

TSLM

Regresses daily MW on a linear trend, weekday indicators, and two pairs of annual Fourier terms.

trend + weekday + Fourier(K = 2)
Compare on paired targets

All candidates forecast every lead through the longest horizon. CV marks the lowest-MAE candidate for each selected region and horizon. Model structures stay fixed for holdout.
